# Finding a Job


If I were to find a programming related job, I would want to work on a company that's working for something I actually value.

That means I shouldn't look for job openings, but instead find the companies I want to work for and ask them for any job openings.

This makes my work be something I'd be passionate about, which will make me be more productive and fulfilled by my work.
