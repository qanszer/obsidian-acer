# Foot Shoe Size


**Common EU Size Conversions:**

- **Size 38:** Fits a foot length of about 24.0 cm.
- **Size 39:** Fits a foot length of about 24.7 cm.
- **Size 40:** Fits a foot length of about 25.3 cm.


**Helpful Tips**:

- Measure your feet in the evening because feet swell slightly during the day.
- Wear the type of socks you plan to use with the shoes.
- If your measurement falls between two sizes, choose the larger size.
