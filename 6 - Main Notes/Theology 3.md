1. Noahic Covenant
	- the effect of sin - murder of abel by cain
	- lamech, son of cain - womanizer, murderer
	- people became so sinful in their times that God decided to wipe them out, by appointing Noah, a righteous person, to make an ark and letting the rest of humanity die
	- God used the rainbow as the symbol of his covenant to never wipe out the sinners again by flood
2. Abrahamic Covenant
	- evil continues to plague the world
	- descendants of abraham: jews, muslims, christians
	- circumcision for all of abraham and his descendants is the symbol for this covenant
3. Mosaic Covenant
	- return to the land of Canaan (the promised land)
	- commandments in the torah (first 5 books of bible)
4. Davidic Covenant
	- Saul was the first anointed king of Israel, anointed by prophet Samuel by God
	- King Ishbosheth became the next king, but was not anointed by prophet Samuel by God
	- 12 tribes was created - Israel
	- the covenant was that the Messiah will come from his lineage
5. New Covenant
	- disregarded God's commandments, and defined good and evil based on their own standards; Jesus was the one who corrected them
	- completion of the Old Covenant
	- new covenant: God promises the blessings foretold in the Sermon on the Mount and at the Last Supper to Christ's followers if they remain faithful to Him
