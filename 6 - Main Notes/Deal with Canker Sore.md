# Deal with Canker Sore


I get canker sores once in a while and everytime, I always forget what to do to quickly heal it so I google the same things over and over again. I'm making this note to avoid that.


**Things to Remember**

- Do not bite, squeeze, or lick it to stop re-injuring the tissue
- You can gently put ice directly on it for not more than 3 minutes to reduce the swelling and sting. OR buy an ice pop or ice cream instead
- For faster healing, dissolve 1 teaspoon of salt in 1 cup of water. Swish around the area for 30 seconds before spitting. Doing this should not make the sore sting. Do this 3 times a day, spanned out (waking up, afternoon, before sleeping)
- Most ideal for fastest healing is no contact on the canker sore. But the most ideal mouth position is lips are gently closed, while teeth are not touching but close to each other.
- Food to avoid
	- acidic (oranges, lemons, tomatoes, pasta sauces)
	- sharp (chips, crackers, fried chicken)
	- spicy
	- hot
	- carbonated drinks
- Food to eat
	- soft proteins (scrambled egg, tofu, non coated meat)
	- cold (ice cream, ice pop, ice)
