20 side quests to do alone before 2027

1. ﻿﻿﻿Travel across the world alone

2. ﻿﻿﻿Take yourself out for dinner. No phone, just a notebook.

3. ﻿﻿﻿Go to the cinema alone. Stay in your seat after it ends.

4. ﻿﻿﻿Pick a nearby town you've never visited, no plan just go.

5. ﻿﻿﻿Go a full day with no phone.

6. ﻿﻿﻿Go into nature with only a notebook, and reflect.

7. ﻿﻿﻿Take a long solo walk and record voice notes as you go. Don't edit. Just talk.

8. ﻿﻿﻿Write a detailed vision for your life and read it every morning for 7 days.

9. Train for something physical that scares you a little. (half-marathon, Hyrox etc)

10. Cook a three-course meal just for yourself. Set the table properly.

11. ﻿﻿﻿﻿Keep a handwritten journal for 30 days.

12. Start a 30-day challenge: gym, cold showers, meditation. Pick one and don't break the chain.

13. Write 100 things you're grateful for

14. ﻿﻿﻿﻿A full day with no complaining (role of life). Track how often you catch yourself.

15. ﻿﻿﻿﻿Introduce yourself to 3 strangers in one day. Not networking, just genuine curiosity.

16. ﻿﻿﻿﻿One act of anonymous generosity. Don't post about it (e.g. paying for someone's coffee)

17. ﻿﻿﻿﻿Rawdog somewhere in nature for an hour with nothing to do. Just be there.

18. ﻿﻿﻿﻿Go to a live event alone: concert, comedy, theatre.

19. ﻿﻿﻿﻿Write a letter to yourself from the future.
